name: Photographer proposal placement
description: User-chosen location for owner-only native club and theme proposal actions
---

Keep Propose club and Propose theme in Edit photographer profile, beside their respective selection controls. Do not show them in the public Photographer detail view, even to the owner. This applies to proposals from the photographer profile; photo upload and photo edit have their own proposal entry points.
Keep the general Send feedback link below Save and Cancel, rather than beside Propose club.

**Why:** The user explicitly corrected the proposal placement after those actions were added to the owner’s public-facing Photographer detail page. Proposal actions belong with editing profile associations, not viewing the profile. They then asked to separate Send feedback from the club proposal, preferably beneath the edit form actions.

**How to apply:** When changing native profile or detail-page parity, leave the detail page free of proposal links and keep both actions available to the linked owner in profile edit mode. Treat general feedback as a separate action after Save and Cancel.
