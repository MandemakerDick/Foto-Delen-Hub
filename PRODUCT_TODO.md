# Product improvement to-do list

## Recommended next

- [ ] **Prepare the Gallery for a growing photo library**
  Move combined filtering, sorting, and pagination to the API, then add infinite scrolling or page navigation so performance remains strong with thousands of photos.

- [ ] **Make AI theme review faster**
  Add filters for review status and confidence, bulk accept/reject actions, and context from similar previous decisions.

- [ ] **Prevent duplicate photos during import**
  Detect identical or near-identical images and let the importer skip, replace, or deliberately keep them.

## Further improvements

- [ ] **Create curated Gallery collections**
  Let admins assemble named selections such as “Photo of the Month” or “Exhibition Selection” without creating artificial themes.

- [ ] **Measure AI theme-assignment quality**
  Report correct and incorrect review outcomes by theme and confidence range so the fixed 85% automatic-assignment threshold can be evaluated with evidence.

- [ ] **Give members notification preferences**
  Let photographers choose immediate alerts, a daily or weekly digest, or in-app-only notifications for reviews, comments, invitations, and theme activity.
